Packaging is the four questions in order: does the product touch it, would that contact matter, what are the contact parts made of, and which polymer. Green means it does not matter or the contact material is inert; orange and red mean it does, scaled by what is inside and how it is used. Grey means a question is still unanswered, which is not a pass. Checks is formula, packaging, recalls and lawsuits, independent tests, in that order. Purple means a recorded finding with a source; amber means it was inferred from a product note and cannot say where it came from; empty means nobody has looked. This page reads brand-data.json live, so it shows whatever was last deployed. The password hides this view, not the data: brand-data.json is served publicly because the extension and the app both read it.
