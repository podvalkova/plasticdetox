If you do nothing else

Four swaps cover most of it

1
Get plastic out of food
No microwaving in plastic, no hot food in plastic. Move to glass and steel.
2
Ditch the nonstick pan
Cast iron or stainless. This targets PTFE and PFAS head on.
3
Filter your tap water
Drink from glass or steel and skip plastic bottled water.
4
Go fragrance free
Fragrance is the biggest hidden phthalate source you own.
Start three to six months out plasticdetox.org
