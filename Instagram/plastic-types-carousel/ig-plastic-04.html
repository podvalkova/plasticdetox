Avoid

Polystyrene (#6)

Styrene migrates into hot or fatty foods
Classified by IARC as possibly carcinogenic to humans (Group 2B). Migration jumps with temperature, oil, and acidity. The hot coffee in a foam cup is the textbook bad case.
Where it hides
Foam coffee cups
Takeout clamshells
Foam meat trays
Packing peanuts
Disposable cutlery
Foam plates
