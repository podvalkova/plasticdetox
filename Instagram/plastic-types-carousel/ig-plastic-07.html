Cleanest swaps

Build a kitchen that lasts

Glass
For storage, microwaving, baby bottles. Inert, reusable, no leaching, no off gassing.
Stainless steel
For water bottles, food storage, cookware. 18/8 or 18/10 grade. No coatings to scratch.
Cast iron
For stovetop cooking. Adds bioavailable iron. Lasts generations. Lodge is the budget pick.
Platinum cured silicone
For bottle nipples, baking mats, storage. Medical grade is the spec. Heat stable to 400F.
Wood and ceramic
For boards, mugs, plates. The lowest tech swap and often the most effective.
