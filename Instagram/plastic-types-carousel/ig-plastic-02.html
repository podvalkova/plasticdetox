First, find the number

Look for this symbol

1
Flip the item over. Usually stamped on the bottom of bottles and tubs, or on the side of bags.
2
Find the small triangle of three chasing arrows, stamped into the plastic, not printed.
3
The number inside the triangle is 1 to 7. That is the resin code.
No number? Treat as #7. plasticdetox.org
