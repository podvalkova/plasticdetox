The 30 Second Summary

  • A plastic cutting board sheds 14 to 71 million microplastic particles per year. Knife grooves shed polyethylene fragments straight into your food (Environmental Science & Technology, 2023).
  • Wood is actually more sanitary than plastic. UC Davis research showed bacteria die off in wood fibers, while knife scarred plastic boards trap bacteria that survive even dishwasher cycles.
  • Best heirloom pick: John Boos Maple End Grain, sustainably sourced hard maple, lasts decades with proper care.
  • Best everyday pick: Teakhaus Edge Grain Teak, naturally water resistant, no constant oiling needed.
  • Best for knife edges: Yoshihiro Hi-Soft Rubber Board, what professional sushi chefs use, dishwasher safe and resurfaceable.
  • Oil monthly with food grade mineral oil. Never olive or coconut, those turn rancid. A wood board you maintain outlasts any plastic board you replace.
