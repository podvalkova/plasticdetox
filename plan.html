Our approach

We do not tell you to replace everything.

Most guides hand you a hundred swaps and let you drown. We rank every change by how much it actually reduces your exposure and impact, so you start where it matters and skip what barely moves the needle. Where it helps, we give a pick for every budget, from the cheapest way in to the best. Every product is vetted on five fronts: formula, packaging, independent tests, lawsuits, and recalls, and we take no money from brands in exchange for a recommendation.

Why this plan starts in the kitchen.

What you eat and drink and what you breathe are the two biggest routes into your body, and heat is the single biggest multiplier on the food side. Hot food in plastic, microwaving, and hot drinks through plastic release far more than the same plastic cold. We start in the kitchen and your water because those swaps are the cheapest and highest certainty wins, then move to the air you breathe in Phase 2. Watch for the Heat driven tag on the swaps that matter most.
