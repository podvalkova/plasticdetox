Bathroom Guide

Greenwashing Claims
vs Reality

Five common bamboo toothbrush labels, decoded
The claim
"100% biodegradable"
The reality
Handle: yes. Bristles: no.
The claim
"Plant based bristles"
The reality
Castor oil nylon. Still a polymer.
The claim
"BPA free bristles"
The reality
Nylon never had BPA. Meaningless.
The claim
"Eco friendly alternative"
The reality
Handle is. Bristles are not.
The claim
"Zero waste"
The reality
Nylon bristles plus staples equal landfill.
A bamboo handle eliminates ~90% of the plastic by weight,
but "less plastic" is not the same as "plastic free."
Read the bristle ingredient, not the front of the package. plasticdetox.org
