How these work as Reels (no filming needed):
  • Tool: CapCut (free) or Canva video. Set canvas to 9:16.
  • Animation: Each element appears one at a time (text pop, scale up, slide in). CapCut has one-tap presets for all of this.
  • Audio: Use trending sounds from the Reels library. Dramatic reveals work best with suspense/bass drop sounds.
  • Length: Keep under 15 seconds. Shorter = more replays = better reach.
  • Batch it: Pick one format, make 6 to 8 variations in one sitting. Schedule with Meta Business Suite. That's a month of content in an hour.
  • CTA variety: Rotate between "Save this," "Tag a friend," "Comment if you knew," "Follow for more." These drive engagement signals.
