BPA Guide

How to Avoid BPA
in Every Room

1
BedroomOrganic bedding, no foam
  • Switch to organic cotton or linen sheets
  • Avoid memory foam with phthalates
  • Remove scented candles and air fresheners
2
Living RoomNatural fiber furniture
  • Replace vinyl flooring with hardwood or tile
  • Use a HEPA vacuum to capture dust
  • Decline thermal receipts or wash hands after
3
KitchenGlass storage, no cans
  • Never heat food in plastic containers
  • Choose glass jars over canned foods
  • Replace plastic wrap with beeswax wraps
4
BathroomPlastic free personal care
  • Switch to fragrance free products only
  • Replace PVC shower curtain with fabric
  • Use castile soap as a multi purpose cleaner
BPA is everywhere. Here is how to reduce it. plasticdetox.org
