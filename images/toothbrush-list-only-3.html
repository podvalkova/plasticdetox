Bathroom Guide

6 Ways to Cut Plastic
From Your Toothbrush

5
Demand FSC Certified Bamboo Skip vague "sustainable" claims
  • FSC verifies responsible forest management
  • Most budget brands have no certification
  • Moso bamboo is the ideal raw material
6
Repair Instead of Replace (Electric) Aluminum body, replaceable heads
  • Conventional electric brushes are pure e-waste
  • SURI 2.0 has a free head recycling program
  • 40+ day battery life, B Corp certified
Most "eco" bamboo brushes still use petroleum nylon bristles. plasticdetox.org
