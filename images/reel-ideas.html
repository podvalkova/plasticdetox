Should I buy a bamboo toothbrush?
The handle is compostable bamboo, but the bristles are still nylon (plastic). Labels saying "100% biodegradable" are misleading because the bristles and metal staples go to landfill. It is a better choice than fully plastic, but not what the marketing implies.
Source: Bamboo toothbrush article, material breakdown

Better than fully plastic, but not zero waste.

The handle is compostable bamboo. The bristles are nylon, a plastic polymer that does not biodegrade. Metal staples holding the bristles also go to landfill. The claim applies to one part of a multi-material product.

The reality
One of three components is biodegradable.
The claim applies to the handle only.

BPA Free means no bisphenol A specifically. Most BPA Free products use BPS or BPF instead, which a 2024 comparative study found have similar estrogenic and anti-androgenic activity. The EU banned all bisphenols in food contact materials. The US has not.

Production Notes

No Filter Talk

Collect questions from DMs, comments, and story polls. Answer 6 to 8 in one sitting. Each follows the same three-frame structure: question, answer, takeaway. Consistent format builds recognition.

The Fine Print

Photograph packaging claims when you see them. Each photo becomes a reel: claim, evidence, reality. The packaging does the visual work. Your research provides the credibility.

From existing articles

Every article has 3 to 5 reels in it. Pull the most cited statistic, the most common misconception, and the clearest swap recommendation. That is three reels per article.

Visual consistency

Use your existing brand elements: Inter font, purple accents, numbered badges, white cards on warm background. Keep the sketch illustrations as backgrounds where relevant. Consistency builds trust.
