Beginner Guide

How to Start Reducing
Plastic Exposure at Home

1
Stop microwaving in plasticUse glass or ceramic dishes instead
  • Heat releases chemicals 5x faster into food
  • Even "microwave safe" plastic still leaches
  • Glass containers work for storage and reheating
2
Replace your plastic kettleSwitch to stainless steel or glass
  • Plastic kettles release millions of microplastics per boil
  • Stainless steel or glass kettles last longer
  • Same goes for plastic coffee makers
3
Ditch non stick cookwareUse cast iron, stainless, or ceramic
  • Teflon coatings contain PFAS (forever chemicals)
  • Scratched pans release particles into food
  • Cast iron and stainless last a lifetime
