Personal Care Guide

6 Swaps to Remove Plastic
from Your Routine

1
Bamboo ToothbrushReplace plastic handle
  • Plastic toothpaste releases 4,000 particles per brush
  • Switch to a natural toothpaste like Dr. Bronner's
  • Try Bite toothpaste tablets in a glass jar
2
Glass ShampooRefillable bottle or bar
  • One shampoo bar replaces 2 to 3 plastic bottles
  • Bars contain zero synthetic polymers
  • Silicone in conditioner coats hair in plastic
3
Safety RazorReplace disposable plastic
  • Disposable razors are 100% non recyclable plastic
  • Stainless steel razors last a lifetime
  • Replacement blades cost pennies each
4
Natural Soap BarSkip liquid in plastic bottle
  • Body wash contains synthetic polymer thickeners
  • Castile soap works for body, face, and hands
  • Bar soap needs zero plastic packaging
5
Wood CombReplace plastic brushes
  • Plastic brushes shed micro fragments with use
  • Wood and bamboo combs are fully compostable
  • Gentler on hair and scalp than plastic
6
Glass Jar CreamAvoid plastic tubes
  • Dimethicone in moisturizer coats skin in silicone
  • Choose creams with natural oils and shea butter
  • Glass jars are endlessly recyclable
Your skin absorbs what you put on it. plasticdetox.org
