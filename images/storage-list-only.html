Food Storage Guide

4 Best Plastic Free
Food Storage Options

1
Glass JarsBamboo lid, airtight seal
  • Perfect for pantry staples and dry goods
  • Bamboo lids double as small cutting boards
  • 100% plastic free from lid to base
2
Steel CanistersDry goods and pantry
  • Unbreakable and kid safe
  • Zero chemical leaching at any temperature
  • Great for lunches and meal prep
3
Cloth CoversReplace plastic wrap
  • Beeswax wraps mold to any bowl shape
  • Organic cotton, beeswax, and jojoba oil
  • Reusable for up to a year
4
Glass ContainersOven and freezer safe
  • Choose borosilicate for thermal resistance
  • Look for glass lids to go fully plastic free
  • Microwave safe with lid vent open
