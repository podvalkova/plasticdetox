Baby & Toddler Guide

Baby Product Swaps
by Exposure Priority

Critical (14+ hrs/day)
Crib mattress · Baby bottles · Pump parts · Breast milk storage
Ingestion + inhalation routes during sleep and feeding
High (multiple daily exposures)
Formula prep · Food storage · Baby wash + lotion · Diapers + wipes · Crib sheets
Direct skin or ingestion contact, repeated heat exposure
Medium (1 to 4 hrs/day)
Car seat · Everyday clothing · High chair · Teethers + toys · Play mat
Skin + inhalation + mouthing routes, cumulative exposure
Lower (still worthwhile)
Stroller · Baby carrier · Cleaning products · Laundry detergent · Air purifier
Shorter contact, indirect routes. Swap as budget allows.
