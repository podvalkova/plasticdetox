Indoor Air Guide

5 Ways to Cut Microplastics
in Your Air

1
HEPA PurifierRun 24/7 to capture fibers
  • Captures 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns and up
  • Place in bedroom first, where exposure is highest
  • Look for true HEPA H13 with sealed housing
2
Natural FurnitureReplace synthetic upholstery
  • Polyester sofas shed fibers every time you sit
  • Choose cotton, linen, hemp, or leather upholstery
  • Cover synthetic couches with natural fiber throws
3
Wool/Cotton RugsReplace synthetic carpet
  • Synthetic carpet has 2x to 10x more microplastic dust
  • Hardwood or tile flooring is the best option
  • Place natural fiber rugs over synthetic carpet
4
Wood BlindsReplace plastic or polyester
  • Polyester curtains shed fibers with every breeze
  • Swap to cotton, linen, or bamboo window coverings
  • Wash existing synthetic curtains in a Guppyfriend bag
5
MERV 13 FiltersTrap particles in HVAC
  • Standard filters miss most microplastic particles
  • Replace HVAC filters every 60 to 90 days
  • Vacuum with a sealed HEPA vacuum weekly
Most microplastic exposure happens indoors. plasticdetox.org
