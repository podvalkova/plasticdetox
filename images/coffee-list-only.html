Coffee Guide

6 Ways to Enjoy Coffee
Without Plastic

1
Moka PotAll metal, zero plastic
  • Choose stainless steel over aluminum models
  • Bialetti Venus is entirely stainless steel
  • Makes strong espresso style coffee
2
Ceramic Pour OverNo plastic pods or filters
  • Get the ceramic Hario V60, not the plastic one
  • Use unbleached paper filters for safety
  • Ceramic is completely inert at all temps
3
French PressGlass and steel brewing
  • No plastic touches the water or the coffee
  • Borosilicate glass with stainless steel filter
  • Lets natural coffee oils through for full flavor
4
Whole BeansSkip plastic pod packaging
  • A single pod releases billions of nanoplastics
  • Buy beans in paper bags from local roasters
  • Use a reusable steel capsule if you keep a pod machine
5
Organic BeansNo chemical solvents
  • 30 to 40% of conventional beans have pesticide residues
  • USDA organic prohibits synthetic pesticides
  • Roasting does not remove all residues
6
Swiss Water DecafChemical free process
  • Most decaf uses methylene chloride solvent
  • Swiss Water uses only water and temperature
  • Look for the Swiss Water logo on the bag
