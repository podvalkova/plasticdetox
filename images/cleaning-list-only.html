Cleaning Guide

6 Swaps for a Plastic
Free Clean

1
Glass SpraysRefillable glass bottles
  • Most spray cleaners are 90% water in plastic
  • Use Blueland tablets in a glass bottle
  • DIY: equal parts vinegar and water works great
2
Natural BrushReplace plastic scrubbers
  • Plastic sponges shed 6K to 10K particles per use
  • Coconut coir scrubbers are naturally abrasive
  • Wood brushes with sisal bristles last months
3
Cotton ClothsReplace microfiber cloths
  • Microfiber cloths shed plastic fibers every use
  • Swedish dishcloths replace 17 rolls of paper towels
  • Use damp cotton cloths for dusting surfaces
4
Metal BucketReplace plastic containers
  • Plastic bins degrade and shed particles over time
  • Stainless steel or galvanized metal lasts decades
  • Use a cotton mop head instead of synthetic pads
5
Castile SoapOne bar replaces many bottles
  • Works for dishes, floors, tiles, and countertops
  • Made from plant oils with no synthetic surfactants
  • Mix with baking soda for tough grout stains
6
White VinegarGlass bottle, no plastic
  • Kills common bacteria including E. coli
  • Streak free glass and mirror cleaner
  • Do not use on natural stone like marble
