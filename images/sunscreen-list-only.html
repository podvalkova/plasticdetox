Sunscreen Guide

6 Things to Know Before
Buying Sunscreen

1
Go MineralZinc oxide is the safest filter
  • Only zinc and titanium are FDA rated safe
  • Sits on skin, does not absorb into blood
  • Works immediately, no 15 minute wait
2
Skip ChemicalOxybenzone enters your blood
  • Chemical filters detected in blood within hours
  • Homosalate still detectable 21 days after last use
  • Oxybenzone linked to endocrine disruption
3
SPF 30 is Enough97% UVB blocked, sweet spot
  • SPF 50 only adds 1% more protection
  • Higher SPF often means more chemical filters
  • Proper application matters more than SPF number
4
Non NanoReef safe, no penetration risk
  • Non nano zinc stays on skin surface
  • Nano particles can enter waterways and coral
  • Slight white cast is the tradeoff for safety
5
Check Zinc %Look for 15% or higher
  • Zinc oxide provides full broad spectrum coverage
  • Titanium dioxide alone misses deep UVA1 rays
  • Higher zinc percentage means better UVA protection
6
Reapply OftenEvery 2 hours, not just once
  • Most people apply only half the needed amount
  • Reapply after swimming or sweating
  • A well applied SPF 30 beats a poorly applied SPF 50
