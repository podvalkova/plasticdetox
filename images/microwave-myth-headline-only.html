Myth This claim is not supported by evidence

Microwaves use non ionizing radiation, the same category as visible light and radio waves. It cannot damage DNA.

Microwaving actually preserves more vitamins than boiling because cooking time is shorter and less water is used.

The real concern

The container, not the appliance. Heating food in plastic releases BPA, phthalates, and microplastics. Always microwave in glass or ceramic.
