Myth Busting

Sea Salt Is Safer
Than Table Salt

Myth Sea salt actually has the most contamination

A study of 39 salt samples from 21 countries found microplastics in 36 of them. Sea salt had the highest contamination, up to 1,600 particles per kilogram.

Mined rock salt was the cleanest. The contamination is in the salt itself. Switching from a plastic shaker to a glass one does nothing.

What to do instead

Use mined salt. Redmond Real Salt (ancient Utah seabed) tests very low for microplastics. Himalayan pink salt and Celtic gray salt are also good options.
