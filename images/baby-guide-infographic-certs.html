Baby Product Certifications:
What Actually Matters

GG
GREENGUARD Gold
Tested for low chemical emissions including VOCs and formaldehyde. Designed for sensitive populations including children.
Best for: mattresses, cribs, furniture, car seats
GO
GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard)
Entire supply chain certified organic. Bans toxic dyes, formaldehyde finishes, and heavy metals.
Best for: clothing, bedding, swaddles, towels
OT
OEKO-TEX Standard 100
Finished textile tested for 350+ harmful substances. Class I is the strictest level, specifically for baby products.
Best for: clothing, bedding, any fabric product
MS
MADE SAFE
Screened against a database of known toxic chemicals. No animal testing. Covers personal care, baby, and household.
Best for: personal care, cleaning, mattresses
EW
EWG Verified
Meets Environmental Working Group's strictest health criteria. Full ingredient transparency required.
Best for: baby wash, lotion, sunscreen, wipes, diapers
Labels that mean less than you think
"BPA free" often means BPS or BPF instead (equally harmful). "Natural" and "eco friendly" have no regulated definition. "Dermatologist tested" has no safety standard. Look for the certifications above.
