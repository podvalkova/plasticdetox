Mixed Solid bamboo is great, composite is not

Solid bamboo (carved cutting boards, utensils) is safe. The problem is bamboo composite: colorful plates and cups held together with melamine resin.

When heated, these release melamine and formaldehyde into food. The EU has issued multiple recalls since 2020 and several countries have banned them.

What to do instead

Check the ingredient list. If it contains melamine resin, skip it for hot food. Choose 100% silicone or stainless steel for toddler dishes instead.
