Tea Guide

4 Steps to Enjoy Tea
Without Plastic

1
Loose Leaf TeaSafest option, zero plastic
  • Use a stainless steel mesh infuser
  • Whole leaves give better flavor than bags
  • Re steep most varieties 2 to 3 times
2
Ceramic TeapotNo plastic interior parts
  • Retains heat better than glass
  • Choose stoneware or porcelain
  • Avoid plastic handles that contact steam
3
Paper Tea BagsOnly unbleached, no nylon
  • Check for polypropylene heat seals
  • Try the burn test on dry bags
  • Traditional Medicinals and Pukka are safe
4
Skip Plastic BagsBillions of particles per cup
  • Nylon and PET bags shed 11.6 billion particles
  • Hotter water releases even more plastic
  • Silken pyramid bags are pure plastic
Billions of plastic particles per cup. plasticdetox.org
