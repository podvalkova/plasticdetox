Laundry Guide

6 Ways to Stop Washing
Plastic Into Water

5
Natural Fibers Cotton, linen, wool clothes
  • Natural fiber shedding is biodegradable
  • Start with items you wash most: underwear, tees
  • Linen sheds the least and lasts for decades
6
Skip Polyester Biggest microfiber source
  • Polyester sheds 496,000 fibers per wash
  • Acrylic is even worse at 730,000 per wash
  • Recycled polyester sheds just as much
One load releases up to 700,000 microfibers. plasticdetox.com
