Bathroom Guide

Bamboo Toothbrush
End of Life

Three steps to actually dispose of a bamboo brush
Step 1
🔧
Remove Bristles
Use pliers to pull out all bristle tufts.
Nylon → Landfill
Boar hair → Compost
Step 2
🧲
Remove Metal Staples
Small metal clips hold bristles in place.
Metal → Recycle
Step 3
🌱
Compost the Handle
Bamboo is fully home compostable. Or repurpose as a garden marker or stirrer.
Bamboo → Compost
PRIMALS uses a glue free design, so the entire brush composts
with no disassembly needed.
Tossing the whole brush in compost leaves nylon bristles behind. plasticdetox.org
