Is this not just the free articles in a PDF?

No. The free site gives everyone the same picks and leaves you to decide what applies. The plan is built from your answers, includes only the swaps relevant to your home, and sequences them by real exposure risk so you know exactly what to do first.

How does the testing fund work?

We pool testing funds until a round reaches about $2,000, then commission independent lab panels and publish the results. You can watch the progress on the meter above.

What do I actually get for $5?

Three things, for one payment. The full non toxic registry, 123 picks across 12 categories ordered by how much each matters. All 100 of the most popular baby products on Amazon rated Skip, Use Carefully or Good Choice, with the research behind every call. And a swap list built from six questions about your own home, each with a product to buy and the $0 way to do it instead. It opens on every page and stays open.

Do I have to buy everything at once?

No. Your plan is tiered by budget and sequenced over 90 days. You start with the highest impact swaps and work through the rest at your own pace.

What if it is not helpful?

If something is unclear or a pick does not fit your home, email us and we will help you sort it out. The goal is a plan you actually use.
