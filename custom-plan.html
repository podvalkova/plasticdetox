The Check tab

Questions

Good questions, honest answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Detox plan, every swap pick, the product check, and the shop are all free, forever, with no account and no ads. The only thing to buy is optional: the $9.99 Baby & Expecting Package, the same one available on the site, for anyone who wants the full baby registry and rated Top 100 unlocked in one place.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. The whole product database ships with the app and lives on your phone, so a product check works standing in a store basement with no bars. It updates in the background whenever you do have a connection, so a new recall or a corrected verdict reaches you without waiting for an app update.

Is the app's information different from the site's?

No. Every verdict in the app is the same one published at plasticdetox.org. The app is the same research, built for your phone: offline, with your Detox progress saved, and a barcode scanner for the aisle.

I don't have an iPhone. What should I do?

The app is iPhone only for now. Everything it does is also on plasticdetox.org and works the same on any device: start the free 90 day plan, browse the store, or check a product on the site itself.

What if I already started the free plan on the site?

Open the Detox tab in the app and pick up in the same order. It is the same plan, so nothing is lost by switching between the site and your phone.
