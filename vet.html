The four checks

Formula

What the product itself is made of, the ingredient list or the materials, held against the standard behind every verdict on this site.

Packaging

What it is wrapped, lined, and capped with. A clean formula sitting in a leaching package still fails.

Recalls & lawsuits

The FDA enforcement database plus the public record of recalls, class actions, and settlements.

Independent tests

Third party lab results for things like lead, PFAS, and phthalates. Where no testing exists, the card says so instead of guessing.

How it works

1
Name the product

Type the brand and the product. Any store, any label, no link or barcode needed. It does not have to be something you found on Amazon.

2
Database answers are instant and free

We check our reviewed database first. If we already researched the product, that answer is yours immediately and never uses a check.

Live research in about a minute

Automated results are labeled as automated until our team has reviewed them. Once reviewed, the verdict joins the public database, free for the next person who asks.

Where this comes from. Every verdict is drawn from independent lab testing, product recalls, class actions, ingredient labels, and material disclosures, with each source linked so you can read it yourself. Nothing is guessed: where evidence does not exist, the card says so. This is general information to help you shop, not a legal or medical claim about any company.
