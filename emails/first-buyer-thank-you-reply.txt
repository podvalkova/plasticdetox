NOTES ON THE DRAFT

Never signal that the product is new or that the buyer is early. A customer
who has just paid should feel they bought something established. Anya was
explicit about this on 2026-08-26 after an earlier draft led with "you're the
first", which is exactly the wrong message to send someone who just handed
over money.

Written in first person, not the site's usual "we". A one to one note from a
founder should sound like one person wrote it. The broadcast rule about using
"we" applies to list sends, not this.

The feedback ask is framed as routine practice ("I read every reply", "reader
notes are how the picks keep getting better"), which invites a response while
implying an existing readership rather than an empty one.

The access link question stays. If the worker webhook failed, this is how you
find out before they get frustrated and ask for a refund.

Do not add a discount, a survey link, or an upsell. It would cheapen it.
