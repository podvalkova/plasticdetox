Subject: Your Brand Check request: CeraVe

Hi there,

Thanks for requesting CeraVe through Brand Check. Here is what our review turned up.

Our verdict: Careful.

The good news first: CeraVe's everyday moisturizers and hydrating cleansers have never been recalled and test free of benzene. If you already use the plain Moisturizing Cream, you do not need to panic.

Where it gets murkier:

- The acne line. In 2024 the independent lab Valisure found benzene at 5 to 12 ppm in CeraVe's Acne Foaming Cream Cleanser, well above the FDA's 2 ppm limit. Benzene is a known carcinogen, and benzoyl peroxide can break down into it when it gets warm. We recommend skipping the Acne Foaming Cream Cleanser and Wash.

- The FDA's own follow up. When the FDA tested 95 benzoyl peroxide products in 2025, more than 90 percent were clean and CeraVe was not among the six products recalled. So the benzene picture is real but contested, not settled.

- The lawsuits. Parent company L'Oreal is facing six class action lawsuits over the benzene claims, now consolidated in New York and still active in 2026. No ruling yet.

- The formulas. Beyond benzene, several CeraVe products still use parabens, phenoxyethanol, PEGs, and petrolatum. These are common in drugstore skincare but are not what we would call clean.

What we would do: If you like CeraVe, the plain Moisturizing Cream is the safest pick in that range. It is benzene free, paraben free, and fragrance free, with genuinely good ceramides and hyaluronic acid. It is not what we would call clean, though. It still uses an ethoxylated PEG emulsifier (Ceteareth 20, which can carry trace 1,4 dioxane) and dimethicone, a silicone polymer.

If you want a genuinely cleaner swap at about the same price, we like the ATTITUDE Sensitive Skin Oat Lotion (unscented). It is EWG Verified, fragrance free, and uses oat, shea butter, and coconut oil with no PEGs, no silicones, no parabens, and no petrolatum. One heads up: a lot of the usual budget clean picks do not actually hold up. Vanicream still contains PEGs, a silicone, and BHT, and The Ordinary Natural Moisturizing Factors still uses ethoxylated emulsifiers. An EWG Verified brand like ATTITUDE is the reliable way to skip all of that. Either way, avoid the benzoyl peroxide acne products entirely.

Hope that helps you make the call.
