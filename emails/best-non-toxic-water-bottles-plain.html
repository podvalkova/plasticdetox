Quick one from me this week. I spent the last few days digging into how insulated water bottles are actually made, and I did not expect the main finding: most brands seal the vacuum insulation with a pellet that is roughly half lead, tucked under a small cover on the base.

Stanley admitted this in 2024, which you may remember from the headlines. What surprised me more is that Yeti says the same thing in its own manufacturing FAQ, and independent testers have found bottles where that leaded spot was exposed, or covered by nothing but paint.

The good news: a short list of brands seals the same vacuum with no lead at all. So I held every pick in the new guide to both halves of our standard, a lead free seal and no plastic in the drink path, meaning nothing but steel, glass or silicone ever touches your lips. There is a section on tumblers, one on the viral bottles everyone asks about, and a kids section, which exists because roughly 6 million kids bottles and cups have been recalled for detaching spouts and illegal lead.
