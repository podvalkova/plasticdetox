Subject: Your Brand Check request: Millie Moon

Hi there,

Thanks for requesting Millie Moon through Brand Check. Here is what our review turned up.

Our verdict: Careful.

There is a lot to like about Millie Moon on paper, but a couple of active issues stop us short of calling it a clean pick, and on a product that sits against a baby's skin all day we would rather tell you the whole picture.

The genuinely good part:

- It passed the PFAS test. This is the one that matters most for diapers. When Mamavation sent diapers to an EPA certified lab and screened for organic fluorine, the marker for PFAS forever chemicals, Millie Moon came back non detect. Six of the thirty five diapers they tested had detectable fluorine. Millie Moon was not one of them.

- No added fragrance or lotion, and it carries OEKO-TEX certification. The wipes, especially the Pure Water and Sensitive lines, are genuinely gentle and fragrance free.

Why we still say careful:

- An active rash and chemical burn complaint cluster. This is the big one. Since January 2026 attorneys reviewing the brand have collected around fifty parent reports across multiple states describing raw, red, peeling, and blistering skin that a lawyer described as consistent with chemical or contact burns, with the symptoms clearing almost as soon as the family switched diapers. At least one complaint has been filed with the Consumer Product Safety Commission and a class action is under investigation. To be fair, these reports are self reported and unproven, no single chemical has been identified, and diaper rash is common and hard to pin on one product. But the volume and the pattern are more than we can wave away on a baby item.

- A false advertising lawsuit over the chlorine claim. Millie Moon originally advertised a totally chlorine free process and quietly moved to elemental chlorine free, which still uses chlorine derivatives. Two women in Massachusetts are suing Target, alleging the diapers were still being sold as chlorine free after that switch.

- Limited transparency. It is a Target exclusive brand made by Zuru, manufactured overseas, with no published material testing beyond that one third party PFAS screen.

What we would do: If you already use Millie Moon and your baby has had zero skin issues, the PFAS result is real and you are not dealing with the worst offenders. But if you are choosing fresh, or if your little one has sensitive skin, we would pick a diaper that holds the stricter totally chlorine free standard and actually publishes its testing. Coterie and HealthyBaby are our go tos there. Millie Moon's wipes, for what it is worth, are a fine buy on their own.

Hope that helps you make the call.
