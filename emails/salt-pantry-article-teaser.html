Pantry Guide

You salt almost everything you eat. So it is worth knowing that when researchers tested 39 salt brands from 21 countries, about 90 percent contained microplastics.

Sea salt is the worst, because evaporating ocean water concentrates the plastic already in the sea. And here is the twist: the trendy natural salts that feel like the safe choice, grey Celtic and pink Himalayan among them, have tested high for lead and arsenic. Lower plastic does not automatically mean cleaner.

Sugar, rice, flour, and honey carry their own loads. The good news is that staples are one of the cheapest, easiest places to cut your exposure, and you only have to make the decision once.
