Subject: Your Brand Check request: PILLAR Performance

Hi there,

Thanks for requesting PILLAR Performance through Brand Check. Here is what our review turned up.

Our verdict: Good choice.

PILLAR is one of the cleaner supplement brands we have looked at, and it clears the bar on the two things that matter most.

Why we like it:

- Full label transparency. Every ingredient and dose is listed on the label. There are no proprietary blends hiding the actual amounts, which is where a lot of sports and wellness supplements lose us.

- Independently batch tested. The core products, including Triple Magnesium and Ultra Immune C, are Informed Sport certified. That means every batch is sent to an independent lab and screened for heavy metals, contaminants, and more than 250 banned substances. For a supplement, third party batch testing like this is the gold standard, and most brands never do it.

- Clean formula. It uses TRAACS chelated magnesium, a well absorbed patented form, and is sweetened with stevia rather than sucralose or aspartame, with no artificial colors. No red-flag fillers.

- No lawsuits or recalls that we could find, and no FDA warning letters.

One caveat on the proteins:

- The whey and plant proteins are the one place we would slow down. Both are Informed Sport certified, but that testing only screens for banned substances and label accuracy, not heavy metals, and PILLAR does not publish any third party lead or cadmium results for them. Protein powders carry an inherent heavy metal risk as a category, and plant proteins are the worst offenders because pea and other plant sources concentrate lead and cadmium from the soil. So the Plant Protein is the one we would flag hardest for anyone who assumes plant automatically means cleaner. The whey also contains both milk and soy, worth knowing if you avoid either. None of this makes them unsafe, it just means they are not verified on the metals question the way we would want before calling a protein genuinely clean.

One more honest heads up:

- Packaging is plastic. The powders come in plastic tubs. The powder is dry, not an acidic liquid sitting in the plastic, so the microplastic exposure is far lower than something like water or oil stored in plastic, but it is worth knowing if plastic free packaging is your priority. If it is, decant into a glass jar at home.

What we would do: If you are taking it for magnesium or immune support, PILLAR is a genuinely good pick. Buy the certified products (look for the Informed Sport logo on the specific product, since not every SKU carries it), and move the powder into glass once you get it home if you want to sidestep the plastic tub.

Hope that helps you make the call.
