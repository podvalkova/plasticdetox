Subject: Your Brand Check request: Odele

Hi there,

Thanks for requesting Odele through Brand Check. Here is what our review turned up.

Our verdict: Good choice.

Odele is one of the cleaner affordable haircare lines we have looked at, and it holds up on the things that matter.

Why we like it:

- A serious free from list. No sulfates, no parabens, no phthalates, no formaldehyde releasing preservatives, and no synthetic fragrance. They also formulate to the EU standard, which bans over 1,300 ingredients that are still legal in US products, so the bar they hold themselves to is higher than most drugstore brands.

- Fragrance is disclosed and essential oil based, rather than a mystery parfum. If you react to fragrance at all, that transparency matters.

- Leaping Bunny certified and fully vegan.

- The dry shampoo dodged the benzene problem. This is the big one. In 2021 and 2022 independent testing found benzene, a known carcinogen, in a long list of aerosol dry shampoos, which led to major recalls. Benzene rode in on the aerosol propellant. Odele's dry shampoo is a non aerosol pump, so it was never part of those recalls and tested clean. If you are switching away from a recalled aerosol, this is a safe landing spot.

- No recalls, lawsuits, or FDA warnings against the brand.

One honest heads up:

- Packaging is plastic. The bottles are plastic, not glass or aluminum, and there is no refill program yet. The formulas are clean, but if plastic free packaging is your top priority, this is the trade off.

What we would do: For an everyday shampoo, conditioner, or a genuinely clean dry shampoo without the benzene worry, Odele is an easy recommendation, especially at its price point. Recycle the bottles when you are done, and you are in good shape.

Hope that helps you make the call.
