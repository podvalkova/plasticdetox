How the verdicts work

Good choice

Careful

Fine in some situations, with real caveats: one line to avoid, mixed test results, or an open question.

Skip

Common questions

Is Product Check free?

Yes. Searching the database of 950+ reviewed brands is free, with no account. If we have not reviewed a brand yet, you can run an instant automated check (packs start at $5 for 20 checks) or request a free hand review, usually answered within 2 business days.

Where do the verdicts come from?

Every verdict is drawn from our published research, which cites independent lab testing from sources like Consumer Reports, Lead Safe Mama, and Valisure, along with product recalls, class actions, ingredient labels, and material disclosures. Each result links to the article behind it.

What do Skip, Careful, and Good Choice mean?

Good Choice means materials, certifications, or independent testing back the product up. Careful means it is fine in some situations but comes with real caveats, like one line to avoid or mixed test results. Skip means it failed independent testing, was named in a recall or lawsuit, or is built from materials we steer away from.

Do brands pay to be listed or rated?

No. There is no paid inclusion and no sponsored placement. Some Good Choice results include affiliate links, and they never influence a verdict: plenty of brands we earn nothing from rate Good Choice, and brands we could earn from rate Skip.

How is this different from the browser extension?

Same verdicts, different place. This page works in any browser on any device. Our free Chrome extension shows the verdict directly on Amazon search results and product pages while you shop, so you do not have to switch tabs.
