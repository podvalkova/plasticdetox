How the verdicts work

Good choice

Materials, certifications, or independent testing back it up. What we would actually buy.

Careful

Fine in some situations but with real caveats: a specific line to avoid, mixed test results, or a pending question.

Skip

Failed independent testing, named in a recall or lawsuit, or built from materials we steer away from.

Where this comes from. Every verdict is drawn from our own published research, citing independent lab testing, product recalls, class actions, ingredient labels, and material disclosures. Each result links to the article it came from so you can read the full reasoning. Brands we have not reviewed yet return an optional AI summary that is clearly labeled and not verified by our team. This is general information to help you shop, not a legal or medical claim about any company.
